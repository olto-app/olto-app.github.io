Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026 · applies to the olto iPhone app, beta included.

The short version

Your training stays on your iPhone. olto has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared. Data leaves the phone only through services you switch on yourself, listed below.

What olto keeps, on your iPhone

Workouts you record or import (time, distance, route, heart rate, power, cadence), the notes and ratings you add, your gear and its use, your plans and settings, and the health readings you allow it to read. It is stored in the app's own storage on the device. The on-device reading and narration run on the iPhone itself.

Apple Health

With your permission olto reads workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and related readings, and writes the workouts it records. You choose each type in the Health permission sheet and can change it any time in the Health app. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves the device through olto, except through a service below that you switch on.

What can leave the phone, only if you switch it on

iCloud backup

A backup archive of your olto data in your own iCloud storage, readable only by your Apple account.

intervals.icu

With your own API key: activities you choose to send, or activities and wellness readings brought in from your intervals.icu account. The key stays in the iPhone's Keychain.

Weather

The workout's location is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the weather at the start. Open-Meteo needs no account and receives no identity.

Sensors

Heart rate straps, power meters and Polar devices connect over Bluetooth, directly to the phone.

Location

Location is used to record routes and, if you allow it in the background, to notice a workout you forgot to start. It stays on the phone except for the weather lookup above.

Beta testing

In the TestFlight beta, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Those are handled under Apple's TestFlight terms and used only to fix the app.

Your control

Turn any connection off in olto's settings, revoke Health or location access in iOS Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on the phone. An iCloud backup can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

olto is not directed at children under 16.
